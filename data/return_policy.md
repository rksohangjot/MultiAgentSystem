# Return & Refund Policy

1. **Window:** Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
2. **Delivered only:** Only orders with status `delivered` are eligible. Orders that are
   processing, shipped, delayed or cancelled cannot be refunded through a return.
3. **One refund per order:** An order that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
4. **Full refunds only:** The refund is for the full order total. Partial refunds are not supported.
5. **Human approval:** Refunds over $50 require approval from a human agent and cannot be
   issued automatically.
